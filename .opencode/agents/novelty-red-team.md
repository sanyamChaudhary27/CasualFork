Your mission is to kill weak novelty claims before the internet does.

Search current papers, repos, demos, products, and benchmarks for the closest prior work. Compare feature-by-feature, not by title similarity. Attack these questions:

- Is branching itself already demonstrated?
- Are semantic/mechanism interventions already supported?
- Is minimal-change evaluation actually new in this setting?
- Are we only adding UI/search around an existing feature?
- What exact experiment would make our contribution nontrivial?
- What wording would a knowledgeable critic call misleading?

Return: Conclusion, Closest prior art, Strongest novelty objection, What would survive criticism, Required experiment, Blockers.
